# H-002 Real 2D roof loop on Colab A100 (4 iterations, not converged)

Changed: the loop from [[H-001-opera-review-deduplication-and-a100-loop]] did no training. It also ran V3/V4 on the building bbox instead of the polygon. It is replaced by a georeferenced partial-label dataset, an RGB multi-task UNet (roof, outline, corner), a whole-AOI scanner with V1..V6 on every polygon, and V1..V6-gated pseudo roofs [REPO: `9200c08`, `a765d96`].

Evidence:
- The A100-SXM4-40GB ran 40 + 3×12 epochs at about 15 s per epoch. The VM was stopped afterwards [CALC: `loop_convergence_report.json`, 2026-10-30].
- Best iteration is 1. On 31 val ADMIS buildings: mean IoU 0.66, median 0.83, 13% at IoU ≥ 0.90, 71% at IoU ≥ 0.50. Pixel IoU on val crops is 0.83 [CALC].
- Iterations 2–4 with pseudo roofs reached val mean IoU 0.52 / 0.53 / 0.58 [CALC].
- Iteration 1: 567 proposals, 118 pass V1..V6, 44% of vertices at 45/90°, 227 overlaps after regularization, 100 touch NoData, 3 of 12 hard negatives hit [CALC].
- AJUSTARE targets: 26/31 detected. The visual sheet shows about 9 plausibly whole roofs, 7 merged with neighbours and 5 missed. OPT_B_205 passes V1..V6 while misplaced [CALC: visual check of `sheet_targets_before_after.jpg`].

Risks: none of the convergence targets were met (IoU ≥ 0.90, 98% ortho, 0 NoData, 0 overlaps). V1..V6 admit wrong shapes, so they cannot gate pseudo labels alone. The VM lacked `laspy` (`stratum_ro/core/__init__.py` imports it).

Next step: owner reviews `ajustare_targets_after` in QGIS. Candidate fixes are a planar overlap resolver after regularization, more owner labels in the north strip, and SAM 2.1 prompts from proposal boxes for merged buildings.
